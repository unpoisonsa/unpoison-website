In August 2023, UnPoison published a public list comparing South African highly hazardous pesticides with the FAO/WHO Joint Meeting on Pesticide Management criteria and EU bans. It was an early step in making regulatory information available to the public.

The accompanying [corrected press release](/documents/hhp-database-2023-press-release.pdf) explains UnPoison's method and its call for government action. The list is a **2023 snapshot**, not a current registration register. For the later edition, see the [2025 update](/resources/hhp-database-2025/).
