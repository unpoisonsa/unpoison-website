This submission argues that South Africa needs a fit-for-purpose regulatory pathway for biological pesticide products.

## Why the category matters

Applying a conventional chemical registration process to biopesticides can slow access to safer alternatives. UnPoison proposes recognising biopesticides as a distinct regulatory category and developing a targeted registration guideline.
