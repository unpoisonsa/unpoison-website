UnPoison's media release describes the Department of Agriculture's multi-sector Pesticide Policy Framework Colloquium in Stellenbosch in August 2025, including participation by civil society, farm-worker organisations, academics, government, farmers and industry.

The release records commitments discussed at the event. It does **not** establish that every commitment has since been implemented. The [original colloquium programme](/documents/pesticide-policy-colloquium-programme-2025.pdf) is preserved alongside it.
