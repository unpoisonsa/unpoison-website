UnPoison's memorandum questions the clarity of the April 2024 derogation guideline, the information provided for risk assessment, and the conditions for meaningful public comment. It asks the Registrar to suspend the process while those issues are reviewed.

This is UnPoison's legal and policy argument from March 2025, not a ruling on the status of the derogation process today.
