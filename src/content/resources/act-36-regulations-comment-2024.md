This submission comments on the 2024 draft regulations relating to agricultural remedies. UnPoison acknowledges changes from earlier drafts while arguing for clearer definitions, enforceable safeguards, public access to registration information, participation in decisions, and a suitable pathway for biological products.

It records UnPoison's position **at the time of the 2024 consultation**. It should not be read as a description of the current legal position.
