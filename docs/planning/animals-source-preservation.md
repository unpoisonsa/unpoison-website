## Original page structure and dependencies

1. **UnPoison Our Animals / Protecting our pets, animals, and wildlife.** Intro to primary poisoning, secondary rodenticide poisoning, symptoms, urgent help, and poison alternatives.
2. **Signs of Poisoning in Wildlife.** Behavioral signs: daylight exposure; remaining in one spot; grounded bird of prey; unusual tameness; lethargy or disorientation; weak or absent flight; sitting near water. The source advises a licensed wildlife rehabilitator or vet and includes vitamin K1 claims that require clinical review.
3. **Report Wildlife Poisoning.** Link to <https://africanwildlifepoisoning.org/>. This is a data-reporting database, not an emergency contact.
4. **Wildlife Rescue List.** Legacy link `/wildlife_and_raptor_rehab_centres_south_africa/` led to a PDF. The PDF is copied unchanged to `public/documents/wildlife-and-raptor-rehabilitation-centres-south-africa.pdf`; directory contacts need verification. Local redirects are drafted.
5. **Secondary Rodenticide Poisoning.** Prey-to-predator pathway, list of affected animal groups, delayed harm, and a linked owl-poisoning short: <https://youtube.com/shorts/p5dA7RdPmxo?feature=share>.
6. **Webinar: UnPoison Our Owls & Raptors.** Embedded YouTube video <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S5eLo2fjfbk>, with examples from Keurboom Park and Cape St Francis and claims about local retailers, electric traps, Cape Town bait stations, and biodiversity work. Current status of these claims needs checking.
7. **Alternatives to Rat Poison.** Sanitation, sealed food waste and bins, enclosed compost, bird feeders, and specific T-REX snap-trap/bait-station recommendations. Product claims and safety guidance need review.
8. **UnPoison Our Wildlife.** Invitation to join as a champion, with tools, templates, resources and a community. The original Google Form <https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfnQLLrH1aXaohLnGUWJ4gk3p6BWtwwXb-otIwJQiXNPsP5sA/viewform?usp=header> still opens as “UnPoison Champs SignUp Form”. It offers Suburb Champ (herbicide spray-list work), Thematic Champ (including pets), or both. Confirm owner, notification destination and campaign relationship with Anna.
9. **FAQs.** Three original questions: approach/capture a poisoned owl or raptor; transport an injured owl or raptor; administer a homeopathic treatment to a poisoned bird of prey. The first two are represented on the local page but require professional review. The third is represented as an *unverified claim*, not treatment guidance; its original dosage/procedure is deliberately not published in the draft. The original WordPress page remains the source for exact wording until a clinician decides what should be published.
